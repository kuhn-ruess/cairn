Habitify

The 12 Week Year can follow your habits in Habitify: a weekly tactic mapped to a habit counts as done when Habitify has it completed on enough days of that week. Cairn reads from Habitify; it writes only when you ask it to create a habit or set a habit's dates to your planned weeks, and only after you confirm the list of changes.

Make a key in the Habitify app under Settings › API (Habitify's API needs a paid plan; a new key replaces the old one there). It is stored encrypted.
