One connection for the whole team instead of one per member. A service token reads the instance once; every ticket is filed under the person who owns it in Zammad — in their own area — and the time booked on it goes to this team's billing, whoever booked it. That is also what closes the gaps a per-member sync cannot: hours on a colleague's ticket, and tickets that open and close between two runs.
